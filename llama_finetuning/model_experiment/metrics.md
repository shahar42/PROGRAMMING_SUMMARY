# Metrics for Evaluating Model Answers

## 1. Accuracy

*   **Correctness:** Is the answer technically correct? Are there any factual errors?
*   **Completeness:** Does the answer cover all the important aspects of the question?
*   **Clarity:** Is the answer easy to understand? Is the language clear and concise?

## 2. Depth of Knowledge

*   **Conceptual Understanding:** Does the model demonstrate a deep understanding of the underlying concepts?
*   **Nuance:** Does the model capture the subtle nuances of the topic?
*   **Connections:** Does the model make connections to other related concepts?

## 3. Explanation Quality

*   **Structure:** Is the explanation well-structured and easy to follow?
*   **Examples:** Are the examples clear, relevant, and helpful?
*   **Analogies:** Does the model use effective analogies to explain complex concepts?

## 4. Conciseness

*   **Brevity:** Is the answer concise and to the point?
*   **Relevance:** Does the answer avoid irrelevant information?

## 5. Style

*   **Tone:** Is the tone of the answer appropriate for the target audience?
*   **Formatting:** Is the answer well-formatted and easy to read?
